## Set-up

Five spots behind the line: two corners, two wings, top of the key. A partner
rebounds and passes; without one, shoot, rebound the miss and return to the same
spot.

## How to run it

Ten shots per spot, 50 in total. Feet set behind the line before the pass
arrives: the shot is the catch, not the dribble. Move after the shot, not before.

## What good looks like

The per-spot percentages sit within roughly ten points of each other, and the
release is identical at every spot. If the corners are far weaker than the top,
that is a footwork finding worth a session of its own.
