## Set-up

At the line, with the same pre-shot routine every time: one dribble, breath,
shoot. The routine is the point. Without it the block measures luck.

## How to run it

Twenty-five shots, two minutes of rest, twenty-five more. Count makes. Do not
chase a miss with a second shot -- a free throw is one shot and the block should
not let a second attempt hide the first.

## What good looks like

The two halves hold within a few percentage points of each other. A first half at
80% and a second at 45% is a conditioning finding, not a shooting one, and it
belongs in the notes of the session.
