## Set-up

Stand still, feet just wider than the shoulders, knees bent. Sequence: right
hand, left hand, crossover, between the legs, behind the back, in front of the
face. Two rounds each.

## How to run it

Thirty seconds on, thirty seconds off, six sets. Count each completed round as a
rep. Do not stop the clock to chase the ball.

## What good looks like

The dribble stays below the knee, the head stays up, and the count per set rises
without the ball leaving the hand. A round dropped in set four is the signal to
stop, not to push on into sloppy reps.
