## Set-up

Start under the rim. The first shot goes up with the right hand off the left
foot, the second with the left hand off the right foot. The ball never comes down
below the chin between shots.

## How to run it

Ninety seconds per block, three blocks. Count attempts and makes. A dropped
rebound ends the block -- there is no recovery, the drill is continuous.

## What good looks like

Both hands look the same, and the run of consecutive makes is long rather than
the total being high. Five makes in a row with the weaker hand is a better result
than fifteen scattered ones.
