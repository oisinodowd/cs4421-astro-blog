## Set-up

Stand a metre from the rim, slightly to one side. One hand under the ball, the
other resting on its side as a guide. No jumping: the block is about the release,
not the legs.

## How to run it

Shoot ten, step back a foot, shoot ten more. Move from the block to the elbow and
back until 50 shots are done. If two in a row miss badly, step back in rather
than shooting the miss into the memory.

## What good looks like

The ball leaves the hand the same way every time, and the misses fall short or
long rather than left or right. A flat, sideways miss is a hand-placement
problem, and it is worth stopping to fix rather than shooting through.
