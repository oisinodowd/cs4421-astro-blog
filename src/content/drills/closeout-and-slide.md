## Set-up

The partner stands beyond the arc with the ball. The cone at the elbow marks
where the closeout has to finish. Three long slides each way, then rest.

## How to run it

Sprint the closeout with short choppy steps, hands up, no reaching. One rep is a
closeout plus two slides in each direction. Six reps per set, three sets, sixty
seconds of rest between them.

## What good looks like

The closeout arrives under control rather than sliding past, and the last slide
of the set is as low as the first. If the chest comes up, the set is over early
and the count stays where it stopped.
