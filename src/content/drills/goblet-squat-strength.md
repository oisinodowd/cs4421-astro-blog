## Set-up

Kettlebell held at the chest, elbows tucked under it. Feet shoulder-width, toes
slightly out. The mat is for the finish, not for the start: this is a squat, not
a hinge.

## How to run it

Three sets of eight, two minutes between sets. Write the weight down. Warm-up
sets at a lighter load do not count towards the total and should not be logged as
working sets.

## What good looks like

Three sets completed at the same weight with the same depth, no rounding in the
lower back and no pause at the bottom that was not there in the warm-up. The
first set that has to be cut short is the ceiling of the weight for now.
