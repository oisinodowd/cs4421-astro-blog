## Set-up

Five or six cones, two metres apart, in a straight line. The drill runs
continuously: finish a run, turn, run it back.

## How to run it

One set is six runs: two at walking pace, two at a jog, two at full speed. Three
sets, ninety seconds of rest between them. A run stops being clean if the ball
touches the floor, bounds off a cone, or the pace drops mid-line.

## What good looks like

The full-speed runs look the same as the jogged ones, with the ball lower. When
the clean runs hold up at speed, move the cones closer together rather than
adding more of them.
